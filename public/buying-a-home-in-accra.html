Review Path

How to buy a luxury home in Accra with more clarity and less noise.

If you are exploring how to buy a house in Ghana, especially in Accra, the process becomes easier when the project gives you a direct line to pricing, plans, site visit coordination, and the practical questions that matter before any commitment.

A Simple Path

A straightforward route from first enquiry to serious review.

1. Request the current pack

Start by requesting guide pricing, current floor plans, and the latest availability position directly from KelDel Court.

2. Review the layout fit

Check whether the three-storey arrangement, master suite floor, and living spaces match the way you actually want to live.

3. Arrange a Private Site Visit

Use the site visit and contact links to coordinate a private site visit so your decision is based on space, location, and finish quality rather than photos alone.

4. Review the details carefully

Once the property is a genuine fit, review plans, specifications, construction status, and any commercial details with the project team.

5. Complete your own due diligence

Before moving ahead, your own legal and financial advisers should confirm the relevant purchase documentation and transaction steps.
