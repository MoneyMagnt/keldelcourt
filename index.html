About KelDel Court

Architecture meets lifestyle in a way that actually feels market-ready.

The strongest asset in this concept is proportion. The project feels premium because the circulation, ceiling height, glazing, and room sequencing are doing the heavy lifting. That gives us a strong brand story to build around: composed living, not performative excess.

Design Narrative

Luxury here is spatial before it is decorative.

The home is organized around a strong sequence: a double-height arrival floor for hospitality, a first floor for everyday family living, and a full second floor reserved for the owner. That logic is rare, memorable, and easy to sell.

The tone of the website should echo that same idea. Clear structure, restrained copy, and strong imagery will make the brand feel intentional rather than busy.

  • Double-height living and dining arrival
  • Fully fitted gourmet kitchen with strong visual identity
  • Three en-suite family bedrooms on the first floor
  • Private office, lounge, terrace, and spa bathroom upstairs

First Floor

Signature Spaces

Bedroom suites The first floor should be positioned as generous and practical, with enough privacy to support family life without sacrificing the sense of a premium address.
Family lounge The shared lounge adds flexibility and gives the plan a softer domestic rhythm between the formal ground floor and the owner’s level above.
Balcony edge Outdoor connection on this level keeps the home feeling airy and layered, not compressed into interior only spaces.
