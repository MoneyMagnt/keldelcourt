About KelDel Court

A residence shaped around light, privacy, and hospitality.

The plan balances dramatic public space with quiet retreat. Ground floor entertaining areas open with double-height volume, the first floor is dedicated to family suites, and the entire top floor becomes a private master world of its own.

Design Narrative

Luxury here is spatial, not loud.

The strongest idea in the project is proportion. Ceiling height, glazing, circulation, and layered finishes do most of the work, giving the interiors a composed, premium feel before furniture or styling ever enter the room.

The top floor is the signature move: a complete master suite level with bedroom, lounge, private office, walk-in closet, spa bathroom, visitor WC, and a large terrace for open-air living.

  • Double-height living and dining arrival
  • Fully fitted gourmet kitchen
  • Three en-suite bedrooms on the family level
  • Private office and terrace on the master level
